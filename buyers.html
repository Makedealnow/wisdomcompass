Before you buy, slow down. Most bad purchases are not bad products — they are good products bought at the wrong time, for the wrong reason, at the wrong price.

The WisdomCompass filter

In buying decisions, the values most often at stake are contentment, counting the cost, honest motives, and patience. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
