The WisdomCompass filter

In business decisions, the values most often at stake are honesty, fair dealing, reputation, and long-term trust over short-term gain. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
