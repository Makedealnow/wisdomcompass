FAQ

Common questions.

Plain answers about what WisdomCompass is, what it costs, and how it works.

Is WisdomCompass free?

Yes — completely, and it will stay that way. The site is supported by values-aligned partner businesses, never by charging the people who come for guidance. See how that works.

Is this a Bible app?

No. A Bible app helps you look up verses. WisdomCompass helps you make a decision: you describe your situation, and it searches Scripture for the passages, stories, and principles that bear on it — how similar situations were handled, and what a wise next step looks like.

Do I have to be a Christian to use it?

No. Every person carries a sense of right and wrong, and the guidance is written to speak to it. The standard it checks your decision against is a biblical view of right and wrong, presented plainly and without preaching. Everybody is welcome.

Does it tell me what to do?

No — and it should not. It lays out the relevant Scripture, the principles, questions worth sitting with, and a wise next step. The decision stays yours, and so does the responsibility for it.

Is this professional advice?

No. WisdomCompass is spiritual and practical guidance only — not legal, medical, mental-health, financial, or emergency advice. For serious or urgent matters, go to a qualified professional. If you are in danger, contact emergency services or trusted local help immediately.

What happens to the situation I type in?

Your saved history lives only in your own browser, on your own device — we do not keep a copy of your situations. Please do not submit private, sensitive, or identifying details; general terms are enough for good guidance. See the Privacy page.

How is the site supported?

By businesses that support God, family, and country, introduced on the Partners page. Sponsored or commission relationships are disclosed plainly, and partner support never shapes the guidance itself.

Can my business become a partner?

If it stands for God, family, and country and treats people honestly, start with the Partners page, then reach us through Contact.
