The WisdomCompass filter

In life decisions, the values most often at stake are trust, patience with timing, honest motives, and courage to take the next right step. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
