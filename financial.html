The WisdomCompass filter

In financial decisions, the values most often at stake are stewardship, contentment, counting the full cost, and refusing to be ruled by fear or greed. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
