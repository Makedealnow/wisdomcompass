The WisdomCompass filter

In insurance decisions, the values most often at stake are providing for family, honest assessment of risk, and refusing both fear-driven and careless choices. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
