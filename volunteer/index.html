Serve With Us

WisdomCompass is free for every visitor, and it runs on people who believe in what it does. If you are a professional who shares that belief, there is a place for your skill here. Everything done here serves one mission — free biblical wisdom for real-life decisions, within everyone's reach.

Who we are asking

Licensed attorneys. Certified public accountants. And other professionals of every kind who stand on the same foundation this site stands on: God, family, and country. You do not need a title we have thought of in advance. If you have a skill and a willing heart, we want to hear from you.

The first job

Our first need is a specific one. WisdomCompass has reached the point where it must decide whether to become a registered nonprofit organization. Our first attorney or CPA volunteer will help us make that decision well — and, if the answer is yes, will guide the legal and tax steps that follow. Every other professional: tell us your skill, and together we will find the fit.

What volunteering is — and is not

  • Volunteers counsel the site itself, behind the scenes. They never give visitors personal legal, tax, or other professional advice through this site — the guidance here stays Bible-only.
  • There are no set hours. Help as you are able, when you are able.
  • Professional time volunteered here is not tax-deductible. We would rather say that plainly now than surprise anyone later.
  • Every licensed volunteer is verified with their state licensing board before any work begins. That is not suspicion — it is the same care we take with everything on this site.
