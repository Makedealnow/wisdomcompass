What about telling my wife? She doesn’t know how bad it is.

Hannah answers this one from Scripture in about forty seconds. The verses she stands on, the principle under them, and the wiser next step are right below the video.

From Scripture

“a man will leave his father and mother and be united to his wife, and they will become one flesh.”

Genesis 2:24 — Berean Standard Bible

“The wicked borrow and do not repay, but the righteous are gracious and giving.”

Psalm 37:21 — Berean Standard Bible

The principle

One flesh was never built to carry a secret. Psalm 37 points one direction on borrowed money: facing it and repaying it. Facing it honestly is where the way out starts — and it starts at home.

Your wiser next step

  • Tell her yourself, and soon. Sit down together, put the real numbers on paper, and show her the first step of your plan.
