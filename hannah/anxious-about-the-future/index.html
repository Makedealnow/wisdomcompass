What does the Bible say about being anxious about the future?

Hannah answers this one from Scripture in about forty seconds. The verses she stands on, the principle under them, and the wiser next step are right below the video.

From Scripture

“The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; all who follow His precepts gain rich understanding. His praise endures forever!”

Psalm 111:10 — Berean Standard Bible

“He who fears the LORD is secure in confidence, and his children shall have a place of refuge.”

Proverbs 14:26 — Berean Standard Bible

The principle

Scripture meets our fear of what’s ahead with a deeper one — reverence for God. That reverence is where wisdom for the decision starts, and it does not come alone: it carries strong confidence, and a refuge for your children.

Your wiser next step

  • Don’t let fear make the decision. Start with God, pray it through, and talk it over with someone you trust.
