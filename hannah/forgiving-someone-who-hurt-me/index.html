Hannah’s Answers · Forgiveness

What does the Bible say about forgiving someone who hurt me?

Hannah answers this one from Scripture in about forty seconds. The verses she stands on, the principle under them, and the wiser next step are right below the video.

From Scripture

“Then Jesus said, “Father, forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing.””

Luke 23:34 — Berean Standard Bible

“But I tell you, love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you,”

Matthew 5:44 — Berean Standard Bible

The principle

Forgiveness starts at the cross: hanging there, Jesus prayed for the very men killing him. Then he turns that same mercy toward us — love your enemies, and pray for the ones who hurt you.

Your wiser next step

  • Pray for the one who hurt you, and leave revenge with God.
