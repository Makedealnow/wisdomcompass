What does the Bible say about lending money to family?

Hannah answers this one from Scripture in about forty seconds. The verses she stands on, the principle under them, and the wiser next step are right below the video.

From Scripture

“Do not charge your brother interest on money, food, or any other type of loan.”

Deuteronomy 23:19 — Berean Standard Bible

“If you lend money to one of My people among you who is poor, you must not act as a creditor to him; you are not to charge him interest.”

Exodus 22:25 — Berean Standard Bible

The principle

In God’s law, lending to family in need was never a way to profit from their trouble. Help was help — no interest charged, and no squeezing the one who is down.

Your wiser next step

  • If you lend, lend as family: help freely, agree on it plainly, and never squeeze the one who’s down.
