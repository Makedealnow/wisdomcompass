What does the Bible say about saving a marriage?

Hannah answers this one from Scripture in about forty seconds. The verses she stands on, the principle under them, and the wiser next step are right below the video.

From Scripture

“Marriage should be honored by all and the marriage bed kept undefiled,”

Hebrews 13:4 — Berean Standard Bible

The principle

Jesus’ first miracle happened at a wedding in Cana — he honored marriage with his presence. And Scripture’s verdict is plain: marriage is to be honored by all. God calls your marriage worth honoring — and worth fighting for.

Your wiser next step

  • Don’t give up on it quickly. Pray together, and get godly counsel early.
