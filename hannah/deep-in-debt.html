Hannah’s Answers · Money & Debt

What does the Bible say about being deep in debt?

Hannah answers this one from Scripture in about forty seconds. The verses she stands on, the principle under them, and the wiser next step are right below the video.

From Scripture

“If you take your neighbor’s cloak as collateral, return it to him by sunset, because his cloak is the only covering he has for his body. What else will he sleep in? And if he cries out to Me, I will hear, for I am compassionate.”

Exodus 22:26–27 — Berean Standard Bible

The principle

God’s own law wrapped mercy around the person in debt. His cloak came back every night so he could sleep. Debt never put a man beyond God’s mercy — or stripped his dignity.

Your wiser next step

  • Read Exodus 22, pray it through, and talk it over with a believer you trust.
