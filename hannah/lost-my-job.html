Hannah’s Answers · Fear & Hard Times

I lost my job last week and I am scared about money. What should I do?

Hannah answers this one from Scripture in about forty seconds. The verses she stands on, the principle under them, and the wiser next step are right below the video.

From Scripture

“your heavenly Father knows that you need them.”

Matthew 6:32 — Berean Standard Bible

“never have I seen the righteous abandoned or their children begging for bread.”

Psalm 37:25 — Berean Standard Bible

The principle

Jesus spoke straight to people worried about food and clothing: your need is not a surprise to your Father — and it is not ignored. David looked back over a whole lifetime and never once saw the righteous abandoned. A hard week is real — but it is not the whole story.

Your wiser next step

  • Write down what your household needs this month, tell one person you trust the truth, and send the first application.
