The WisdomCompass filter

In family decisions, the values most often at stake are covenant faithfulness, patience, forgiveness, protection of the vulnerable, and wise counsel. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
