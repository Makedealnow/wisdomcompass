Vehicle Decisions

A vehicle is one of the biggest purchases a family makes — and one of the easiest places to be rushed, pressured, or sold more than you need.

Decisions people bring here

Tap any question to take it straight into the guidance tool — or bring your own, in your own words.

  • The dealer says this offer is only good today. How do I slow this down and decide wisely?
  • Should I buy new, used, or keep repairing the vehicle I have?
  • I want the truck, but the payment would be tight every month. Is wanting it enough reason?

The WisdomCompass filter

In vehicle decisions, the values most often at stake are counting the full cost, patience under sales pressure, and buying for real need instead of image. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
