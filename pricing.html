WisdomCompass is free to use — no account, no trial, no tier where the good answers cost money.

How a free site pays its bills

Businesses that support God, family, and country partner with WisdomCompass to be introduced to its audience. Their sponsorship and affiliate support covers the cost of running the site. That arrangement is deliberate: the people who need guidance most are often the people least able to pay for it, and a decision made under pressure should never come with a paywall in front of it.

Two promises come with that model. The guidance is never written to sell you anything, and sponsored relationships are disclosed plainly. And our visitors' information is never sold — partners are introduced through the site, never handed a list.
