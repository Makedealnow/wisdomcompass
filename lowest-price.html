The WisdomCompass filter

In deal decisions, the values most often at stake are contentment, patience, honest value over headline price, and refusing manipulation. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
