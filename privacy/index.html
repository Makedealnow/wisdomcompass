Privacy

Privacy at WisdomCompass

Short version: this site is built to need as little of your information as possible.

What you type into the guidance tool

Describe your situation in general terms. Do not submit private, emergency, legal, medical, financial, or highly sensitive information, and do not include names or identifying details — the guidance works just as well without them. Guidance you choose to save is stored only in your own browser, on your own device, and you can clear it at any time from the tool itself.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages and topics people use, so the library can grow where it is needed. Analytics reports are aggregate — pages visited, general location, device type — not tied to the situations you describe.

Email

If you join the WisdomCompass email list, your address is used only to send the guidance and values content you signed up for, including introductions to aligned partner businesses. Your address is never sold, and partners never receive the list. Every email carries an unsubscribe link.

Contact email

If you write to us, your message is used only to respond to you and to keep ordinary business records.

External links

Passage study links open on external sites such as BibleGateway and BibleHub. Their privacy practices are their own.

Questions about privacy: see the Contact page.
