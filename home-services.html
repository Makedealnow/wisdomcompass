  • A repair salesman says it cannot wait. How do I tell a real emergency from a pressure tactic?

The WisdomCompass filter

In home-services decisions, the values most often at stake are diligence, fair dealing, verifying before trusting, and patience over urgency. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
