Mortgage Decisions

A mortgage is a decades-long promise. It deserves more than a monthly-payment number and a signature at the end of a long closing day.

Tap any question to take it straight to Hannah — or bring your own, in your own words.

  • How much house can we honestly afford, not just get approved for?
  • Should I refinance, or is the lower payment hiding a worse deal?
  • We are house-hunting and I feel pressure to stretch. How do I hold a wise line?

The WisdomCompass filter

In mortgage decisions, the values most often at stake are counting the full cost, contentment with enough house, and keeping a promise your family can actually keep. Whatever the decision, slow it down with five questions:

  • What is the real decision?
  • What facts are missing?
  • Who is affected?
  • What are the risks and long-term consequences?
  • What does wisdom, responsibility, and faithfulness require?
